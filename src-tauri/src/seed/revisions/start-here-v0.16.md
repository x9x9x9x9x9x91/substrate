## What a dashboard is

> [!note] The property does it
> Any note whose frontmatter carries a `dashboard:` property stops rendering as text and becomes a live surface instead. This one is `dashboard: hub` — a page whose body is ordinary markdown, laid out in sections and cards.
> [!idea] Other kinds
> `metrics` puts stat cards over a sheet, `charts` plots a database, and `yield-apr`, `food` and `feed` are small trackers. The demo vault that ships with the app has a working example of each.
