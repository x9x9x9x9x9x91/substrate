Tax-year aggregates, one row per category. Whatever keeps your books — a
spreadsheet, an accounting tool, an agent walking the receipts — writes these
rows; the [[Tax Readiness]] dashboard reads them and never writes back. The
categories and summaries below are examples: name them however your own filing
does, and point the dashboard's cards at the summaries you want on the board.

```csv
category,sheet,rows,amount_eur,basis
Income,Income,38,21400,Business
Business expenses,Expenses,52,9260,Business
Equipment,Expenses,11,1840,Business
Home office,Expenses,12,2100,Personal
Rental,Rental,17,7300,Property
Partnership,Partnership,9,4100,Partnership
```

```formulas
income_ytd = SUMIF(category, "Income", amount_eur)
expenses_ytd = SUMIF(category, "Business expenses", amount_eur)
profit_ytd = income_ytd - expenses_ytd
threshold_headroom = 25000 - income_ytd
equipment_ytd = SUMIF(category, "Equipment", amount_eur)
home_office_ytd = SUMIF(category, "Home office", amount_eur)
rental_ytd = SUMIF(category, "Rental", amount_eur)
partnership_ytd = SUMIF(category, "Partnership", amount_eur)
documents = SUM(rows)
```
