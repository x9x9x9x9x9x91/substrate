Six pots on a west-facing balcony, so everything gets sun from two o'clock and
nothing before it. Third season; the first two were guesswork.

What's in:

- **Tomatoes** — two plants, staked. Last year they went in too late and only
  fruited in September. In on 12 April this time.
- **Mint** — its own pot, deliberately. Shared soil and it takes the lot.
- **Chillies** — bought as seedlings rather than seed. Seed germinated fine
  indoors and then sulked the moment it went out.
- **Thyme, sage** — the two that survive being forgotten for a fortnight.

What failed and why: basil twice, both times from cold nights in May rather
than from watering. It goes out in June from now on, or not at all.

Standing jobs are on [[Errands]] — compost and a longer hose reel. The
soil-and-shade book on [[Reading Log]] is what changed the watering: deep
twice a week beats a splash every evening, which is what the first two seasons
were.
