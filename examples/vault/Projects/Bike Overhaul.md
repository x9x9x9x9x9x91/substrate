The commuter bike, stripped down over a long weekend. It was rideable — it
just cost more effort every month than it should have, and the noise from the
back wheel had stopped being intermittent.

Done:

- Chain and cassette replaced together. The old chain was past the wear
  gauge, and a new chain on a worn cassette skips under load.
- Rear hub repacked. That was the noise.
- Both brake cables replaced; the housing was fine.
- Bar tape, because the bike was in pieces anyway.

Not done: the bottom bracket is creaking under a hard start and wants a tool
this box doesn't have. It's on [[Errands]] as a shop job rather than a
weekend one — the last time a borrowed tool met a stuck part, the part won.

Notes for next time: photograph the cable routing BEFORE cutting anything.
Reassembly took twice as long as the strip-down for exactly that reason.
